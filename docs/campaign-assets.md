# AURA Campaign Assets

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for the storefront redesign. These are illustrative campaign photographs, not verified inventory photography. Catalog placeholders were also replaced with 24 studio product concepts; their complete prompts and saved paths are recorded in catalog-assets.json.

- Desktop: `public/images/aura-silk-campaign.webp` (1536 x 1024, approximately 80 kB)
- Mobile: `public/images/aura-silk-mobile.webp` (1024 x 1536, approximately 70 kB)

The generated originals were encoded as WebP using Sharp, without changing their composition.

## Desktop Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a photorealistic editorial campaign photograph for AURA, a premium intimate apparel and silk loungewear ecommerce homepage. Wide landscape 1536x1024 or wider. A naturally beautiful adult woman around 30 with dark brown hair casually tied back, wearing an elegant opaque pearl-white silk camisole and matching flowing silk lounge trousers, fully clothed, relaxed seated pose on a low pale limestone bench at the RIGHT side of the image. Entire upper body and most of trousers visible, silk clothing clearly inspectable, fine silk sheen and seams. Minimal modern studio interior with muted sage-gray plaster wall extending across the full width, a large softly sunlit window out of frame. Left 55 percent mostly uninterrupted medium-light gray-green wall with subtle natural window shadows, suitable for dark text overlay. Refined fashion catalog art direction, natural skin texture, calm confident expression, tasteful nonsexual commercial fashion photography, realistic fabric, bright daylight, subtle film grain, artful yet clean composition. No text, no logos, no collage, no borders, no split panels, no gradients, no decorative props. This is a single coherent full-bleed photograph, subject anchored in right third.

## Mobile Prompt

An adult woman around 30 with dark brown hair tied back wearing a fully opaque pearl-white silk camisole and matching wide leg silk trousers, seated on a low limestone bench, tasteful nonsexual commercial clothing photography. A coherent sunlit studio with light gray-green sage plaster wall. Composition crucial: camera pulled back, entire woman on the FAR RIGHT side, her face at x=80%, y=20%, her whole figure occupies only the RIGHT 45% of frame and ends at y=80%. LEFT 55% is a continuous empty sage-gray wall, bottom left also empty wall and floor, for overlaying dark green website text. Clothing must be clear and sharply focused, real seams and silk sheen, natural skin, relaxed confident expression. Soft daylight with subtle window shadows upper left only. Modern fashion magazine art direction. No text, logos, graphics, borders, gradients, panels or collage. Single photograph.
